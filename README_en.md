## Philosophy

> **Investigate things, extend knowledge, rectify the mind, make the will sincere, and cultivate oneself.**
>
> Take computer technology as the foundation for establishing oneself in the material world, and use it to support personal growth. **Put people first; let the Dao (principle) guide the technique, and let the technique safeguard the Dao.**
>
> The Wuzuniao series will use AI as an auxiliary tool, developing a variety of products that help with study and life around individual needs; achieving reduced repetitive labor, efficient living, and saving time to pursue more meaningful things. If we are fortunate, the Wuzuniao series will become your companion in study, life, and entertainment. Welcome!
